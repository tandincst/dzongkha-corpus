## What this means

- **Share and adapt:** you may copy, redistribute, and adapt the data for any purpose, including commercial use.
- **Attribution:** you must credit "The Dzongkha Corpus Project contributors", link to the license, and indicate if you made changes. Third-party sources, including Wikipedia, must also be credited (see [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) and the `source` fields).
- **ShareAlike:** if you remix, transform, or build on the data, you must distribute your contributions under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0) or a compatible one.

## Why CC BY-SA

The corpus includes text from Wikipedia, which is licensed under CC BY-SA. Wikipedia text and any adaptation of it must remain under CC BY-SA, so the corpus as a whole uses the same license. Material under CC BY, CC0, or in the public domain can be included in a CC BY-SA collection.

## Third-party material

Some records come from third parties and keep their own attribution requirements. These are identified in each record's `license`, `source`, and `permission_ref` fields. Material under NonCommercial (NC) or NoDerivatives (ND) licenses is not accepted, and material used by permission is accepted only if the permission allows release under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Irrevocability

Creative Commons licenses cannot be withdrawn for copies that have already been distributed. Takedown requests (see the README) remove items from future releases only.
